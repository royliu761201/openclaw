## Dimensions of Audit
1. **Non-triviality**: Is it more than a parameter switch?
2. **Surprise Factor**: Does it defy common expectation?
3. **Substitutability**: Can a simple baseline do this?
4. **Knowledge Delta**: What new theory/insight is generated?
