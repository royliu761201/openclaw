# Standards for High-Quality Grant Applications

## 1. National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC / 国自然)
**Core Value**: **Original Innovation** & **Scientific Basis**.
*   **Target**: "0 to 1" Scientific Discovery.
*   **Key Criterion**: "Encourage exploration, Highlight originality."
*   **What makes it GOOD?**
    *   **Scientific Problem (Key)**: Must be a fundamental scientific question (Why/How), not just an engineering task (What).
    *   **Hypothesis-Driven**: Clear academic hypothesis validated by preliminary data.
    *   **Continuity**: Shows a solid track record (Papers) in this specific niche.
    *   **Feasibility**: "The idea is bold, but the method is solid."
    *   **Specifics**:
        *   *Abstract*: 400 words. Must cover Importance, Content, Method, Key Problem, Expected Result.
        *   *Innovation*: Must differ from the "Prior Art" of others AND your own past work.

## 2. National Major Science and Technology Projects (Major Projects / 科技重大专项)
**Core Value**: **National Strategy** & **Industrial Application**.
*   **Target**: Solving "Choke Point" (卡脖子) technologies and massive industrial deployment.
*   **Key Criterion**: "Goal-Oriented, Full-Chain Integration."
*   **What makes it GOOD?**
    *   **Strategic Alignment**: Must rigorously map to the specific text of the "Guide" (指南). Do not deviate.
    *   **Team Strength**: Requires a "National Team" configuration (Leading PI + Top Industry Partners).
    *   **KPIs (Indicators)**: Hard indicators (e.g., "Latency < 1ms", "Scale > 10M users") are the law. Must be aggressive but proven reachable.
    *   **Impact**: Focus on Economic Value and Social Security, not just citation counts.

## 3. General "Golden Rules" for AI Grants
1.  **The "One-Page" Rule**: The Reviewer must understand your core contribution and why it matters within reading the first page (Project Summary).
2.  **Visual Logic**: Technical roadmap charts must be self-explanatory.
3.  **Budget Reality**: Budget items must match the research tasks exactly (e.g., don't ask for generic GPUs if your task is lightweight logic).
4.  **Consistency**: The "Problem" identified in the Abstract must be the "Problem" solved in the Conclusion.
