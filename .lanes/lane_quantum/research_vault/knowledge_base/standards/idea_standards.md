# Scientific Taste:# Idea Merit Guidelines: The Nobel Filter

When proposing or evaluating a research idea, you MUST answer the following questions. If any answer is vague or missing, the idea is **REJECTED**.

## 1. The Heilmeier Catechism (Elevated)
*   **What are you trying to do?** Articulate your objectives using absolutely no jargon.
*   **How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?** (The "SOTA Trap": Don't just improve; Disrupt).
*   **What is new in your approach and why do you think it will be successful?** (The "Secret Sauce").
*   **Who cares? If you are successful, what difference will it make?** (The "Nobel" Check: Does this change a Paradigm?).
5. **What are the risks?** Why might this fail? (e.g., "ReLU might cause training instability").
6. **What are the mid-term and final "exams" to check for success?** (e.g., "Must beat Llama-3-8B on GSM8K by 2%").

- **Novelty**: Is this a trivial combination of existing methods? (Rejection threshold: High)
- **Feasibility**: Can this be verified with the available compute and data within 24 hours?
- **Falsifiability**: Is there a clear experimental result that would prove this idea WRONG?
