# Academic Writing Standards: Best Oral Selection

## 1. Narrative Arc (The "Oral" Quality)
*   **The Hook**: The first paragraph must identify a *fundamental* gap, not just a marginal improvement.
*   **The Journey**: Structure the paper as a quest. Introduction = Call to Adventure; Method = The Weapon; Experiments = The Battle.
*   **Telegraphing**: Every section must end with a sentence leading into the next. No abrupt transitions.

## 2. Visual Excellence (Figure 1 Rule)
*   **Figure 1 is King**: The first figure must summarize the *entire* contribution in a single glance. It must be "Slide-Ready".
*   **No "Box-and-Arrow" Boredom**: Use semantic icons, gradients, and clear data flows.
*   **Caption completeness**: Captions must be self-contained stories. Reading only figures+captions should explain 80% of the paper.

## 3. Mathematical Rigor & Clarity
*   **Notation Dictionary**: consistent $\mathcal{X}, \mathbf{y}, \theta$ throughout.
*   **The "Why" before "How"**: Never introduce a formula without explaining the intuition *first*.
*   **Theorem Quality**: Lemmas must serve the main theorem. No "math for math's sake".

## 4. Empirical "Overkill"
*   **Baselines**: Compare against the *current* SOTA, not just comfortable baselines.
*   **Ablations**: Dismantle your own method. Prove every component is load-bearing.
*   **Error Bars**: No naked numbers. Mean $\pm$ Std Dev is mandatory.
