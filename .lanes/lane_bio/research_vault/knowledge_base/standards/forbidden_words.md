# Forbidden Words & Phrases (Anti-Detection)
> **Rule**: Do not use these words. Their high frequency in LLM outputs triggers AI Detection tools (ZeroGPT).

## Forbidden Adverbs/Adjectives
- Meticulous / Meticulously
- Comprehensive / Comprehensively
- Delve / Delving
- Pivot / Pivotal
- Testament
- Underscore
- Paramount
- Facet / Multifaceted
- Intricate

## Forbidden Phrases
- "In conclusion" (Use specific transition: "Our results imply...")
- "In summary"
- "It is important to note that"
- "It is worth mentioning"
- "As shown in the figure" (Use: "Figure X illustrates...")
- "A landscape of..."
- "This paper aims to..." (Use: "We propose...")

## Stylistic Constraints
- **Avoid Passive Voice**: "Experiments were conducted..." -> "We conducted experiments..."
- **No Bullet Points in Text**: use full paragraphs for academic flow.
- **Variation**: Ensure sentence length varies (Burstiness). Do not write 5 sentences of equal length in a row.
