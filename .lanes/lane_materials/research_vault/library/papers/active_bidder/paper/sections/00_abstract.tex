\begin{abstract}
Current computational advertising systems predominantly maximize Click-Through Rate (CTR) via reinforcement learning or supervised regression, treating users as passive environmental variables. This approach often leads to adversarial dynamics, such as clickbait and user fatigue. In this work, we propose \textbf{Active-Bidder}, a novel bidding framework grounded in the Free Energy Principle (FEP). By modeling the user as an active inference agent minimizing surprisal, we derive a Variational Free Energy (VFE) objective that naturally balances exploration (epistemic value) and exploitation (relevance). We demonstrate that minimizing the joint free energy between the ad system and the user significantly improves long-term Lifetime Value (LTV) while maintaining competitive short-term CTR.
\end{abstract}
