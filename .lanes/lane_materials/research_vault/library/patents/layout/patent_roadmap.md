## 2. Technical Disclosure Strategy (Non-Abstract Focusing)

1. **Define the "Technical Problem"**: For each, we must specify a problem in the physical or digital domain (e.g., "long-term numerical instability in PDE solvers" or "inaccurate temporal alignment in clinical data").
2. **Describe the "Technical Solution"**: Focus on how the software interacts with data, memory, or physical laws.
3. **Demonstrate the "Technical Effect"**: Use the paper's experimental results to prove hardware efficiency, accuracy gains, or safety improvements.
